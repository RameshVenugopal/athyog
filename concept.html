Why we practice

Most yoga today is noise — loud music, louder promises. We teach the opposite: one breath at a time, one honest posture at a time, until the nervous system remembers what calm feels like.

— Yog Guru Ashwini Patil

Signature program

Twenty-one days.
One new baseline.

Slim Smart Sadhana — detox, strength and food rhythms that survive after the program ends. 5:30–7 AM, Mon–Sat, recordings included.

Read the full program →
Week 01 / 03
i.

Cleanse — days 1–7

Yogic detox practices, gut-kind food rhythms, early nights. Most people report lighter mornings by day five.

ii.

Strengthen — days 8–14

Core-focused asana and metabolism-first pranayama. Stamina climbs; the 5:30 alarm stops hurting.

iii.

Sustain — days 15–21

Lock in the habits you will actually keep — food, sleep, a 20-minute home practice. Leave with a plan, not just memories.
