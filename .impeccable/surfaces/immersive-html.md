## Scope

The immersive concept site (`immersive*.html`, shared `assets/css/immersive.css`, `assets/js/immersive.js`). Visitor mode: Persuade. Visitors are Pune locals and online learners in equal measure. Action: open WhatsApp (trial, batch enquiry, corporate session, donation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the morning sadhana itself. The page is a sky that moves from Brahma Muhurta night to full day as you read. It refuses the category's photo-hero + beige + serif studio template and the dark-neon meditation-app look.

OWN-WORLD: Flat, registered sky bands (night indigo #121735, pre-dawn #262355, dawn violet #5B3A6E, first blush #D4687E, day #F4F6FB); never gradients or glows. Saffron #F29B38 appears only on WhatsApp actions, the one sun. Type is Anek Latin (wide display, Indian foundry) over Mukta (Latin + Devanagari body). Hairline rules instead of cards; every photograph is a plate with a ruled caption. Marks carry state: tick done, flag today, ring ahead.

STORY: The visitor meets the teacher and the practice at its real hour, sees live batches and the 21-day programme, trusts real students' words, and opens WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The night band fills the screen. The headline "Come home to your body." is set large in wide Anek. The lede and the saffron "Book a trial" sun sit bottom-left; the hero video is a framed plate at right with a caption; the header clock reads 04:45.

FORM: Brahma Muhurta, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list; seed fda01585.

Raises: flat bands (from woodblock); saffron as the single stamp (from akari); marks not hues on the 21-day rail (from cutting-bench); ruled captions on plates (from sapeur); one clock drives sky, header time and rail (from algorave).

Signature interaction: the header clock and horizon rule advance with scroll (04:45 → 07:00), in the same rAF tick; reduced motion keeps the static bands.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
